# Catalogue management

Browse, discover, and track your library across several complementary views. All pages are gated by
`favorites.view`/`playlists.view`/`play.view` permissions where relevant — see `CLAUDE.md`'s API/Pages
tables for the full route list.

## Browse (`/browse`)

Artist grid with letter, genre, and match-score filters, infinite scroll. Each artist's page
(`/artist/[slug]`) aggregates releases across any connected (duplicate-merged) artists, shows
MusicBrainz-vs-local match status per release, and exposes sync/scan controls (see
`docs/scripts/sync.md`).

## Explore (`/explore`)

4-slider discovery (energy / era / familiarity / sound) that scores and picks tracks on demand — a
different browsing mode from the static catalogue grid, aimed at rediscovering the collection rather
than looking something specific up.

## Timeline (`/timeline`)

Browse by decade/year across the whole catalogue.

## Statistics (`/statistics` + subpages)

Per-metric dashboards: artists, releases, tracks, genres, bitrate distribution, library size, play
counts, sync/art coverage, incomplete/unmatched releases, shortest releases, and more. Each subpage is
a focused view over one aggregate rather than one giant dashboard.

## Favorites & Playlists

Favorite individual releases/tracks (`/favorites`, tabbed); manual or genre/region-generated playlists
(`/playlists`, `PlaylistType`: MANUAL | GENRE | REGION — genre/region playlists are regenerated by
`./playlists`, see `docs/scripts/playlists.md` if present, or the script's `--help`).

## Labs (`/labs` + subpages)

Experimental/visual views: world map tiled by artist origin country, genre/artist relationship graphs
(genome, network), a generated album-art mosaic, and a decades visualization. Not core browsing — more
for poking at the collection from odd angles.
